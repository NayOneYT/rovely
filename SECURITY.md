# 🛡️ Security Policy

At **ROVELY**, we take the security and privacy of our users extremely seriously. Whether it's authentication, user data, or real-time communications, we aim to maintain high security standards.

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, we deeply appreciate your help in disclosing it to us responsibly.

---

## 🚨 Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please DO NOT report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, or public discussions.**

If you discover a vulnerability, please reach out to us directly through one of the following confidential channels:

- **Telegram:** [@Ivoshen](https://t.me/Ivoshen) *(Fastest response)*
- **Email:** [nayone.tapok@gmail.com](mailto:nayone.tapok@gmail.com)

### What to include in your report:
To help us understand and fix the issue quickly, please provide:
1. **Description** — A brief summary of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
2. **Steps to Reproduce** — Clear steps, proof of concept (PoC), requests, or screenshots.
3. **Affected Components** — E.g., Frontend, Backend API, Socket.IO, Authentication flows.

---

## ⏱️ Response Policy & What to Expect

- **Initial Response:** We will acknowledge receipt of your report within **24–48 hours**.
- **Assessment:** We will verify the issue and determine its severity.
- **Fix & Progress:** We will keep you updated on our progress toward releasing a patch.
- **Credit:** Once resolved, we will gladly acknowledge your effort in our release notes/README (if you wish to be credited).

---

## 🤝 Responsible Disclosure Guidelines

We kindly ask researchers and contributors to:
- Give us reasonable time to fix the issue before sharing details publicly.
- Avoid accessing, modifying, or deleting other users' data during testing.
- Avoid Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS) attacks or automated spam testing on live services.

Thank you for helping us keep ROVELY safe and secure for everyone! ❤️
